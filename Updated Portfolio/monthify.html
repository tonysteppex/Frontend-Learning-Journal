PropTech · Dual-Sided Rental Ecosystem

Monthify Web App

Product Designer

2026

Paying one to two years of rent upfront places a heavy financial burden on tenants across Nigeria, while landlords struggle with unverified tenants and manual rent tracking. I designed Monthify, a dual-sided web application that enables flexible monthly rent payments, verified roommate matching, and automated landlord property management.

01 The Challenge

Bridging The Trust Gap Between Monthly Tenants And Property Owners.

To make monthly rent work in the Nigerian market, the platform had to solve financial flexibility for renters while guaranteeing security and predictable payouts for landlords.

01 / AFFORDABILITY

Rigid Annual Rent Cycles

Renters needed transparent monthly payment breakdowns without hidden agency fees or confusing loan terms.

02 / COMPATIBILITY

Unsafe Roommate Searches

People looking to split rent lacked a safe, structured way to match with verified roommates based on lifestyle and budget.

03 / OVERSIGHT

Fragmented Landlord Tools

Property owners needed a simple dashboard to screen applicants, track monthly collections, and manage occupancy across units.

02 Design Approach

Two Tailored Experiences Powered By One Unified Design System.

I built a scalable component library in Figma that adapts seamlessly across desktop and mobile breakpoints, guiding tenants and landlords through role-specific onboarding flows.

Transparent Monthly Pricing

Present monthly rent, service charges, and verification status upfront on every listing card so tenants can budget with confidence.

Match Verify Move In

Verified Roommate Matching

Design structured roommate profiles highlighting identity verification, shared living preferences, and split-rent calculators.

Landlord Portfolio Hub

Give property owners a clean command center to review tenant applications, monitor automated payouts, and track unit leases.

Selected Platform Screens.

Design Outcome

A Cohesive Rental Experience Built For Trust And Flexibility.

Monthify brings property discovery, roommate matching, and landlord administration into a single responsive web platform. Backed by a complete Figma design system across mobile and desktop viewports, the product makes monthly living accessible for tenants and effortless to manage for property owners.
